FLEXIBLE DEALS

Choose Your Deal Method

Sellers can offer different ways for buyers to complete a deal.

Direct Deal

Buyer contacts the seller directly and negotiates outside the platform. No marketplace commission by default.

Important: Direct deals are handled between buyer and seller. The platform does not control outside payments or guarantee them.

Website Deal

Buyers can place structured orders through the website when ordering and payment features are enabled.

Online payments, commissions and settlements require an integrated payment provider and verified backend setup.
